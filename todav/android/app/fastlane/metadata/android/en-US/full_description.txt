Todav is a task list and calendar kept in your Nextcloud through CalDAV, so the Nextcloud Tasks and Calendar web apps, DAVx⁵, Thunderbird and other CalDAV clients see the same lists and events. It syncs with your Nextcloud by itself: it needs neither DAVx⁵ nor the Nextcloud app. Or keep your lists on this device only, without a server.

<b>Tasks</b>
<ul>
<li>Categories, subtasks nested to any depth, priority, pinned tasks and notes</li>
<li>Due dates with times, start times and several reminders per task, with "Mark as Done"</li>
<li>Upcoming and Important pages across all lists, search and undo</li>
<li>Swipe actions of your choice; drag and drop to reorder tasks, make subtasks or change a category</li>
</ul>

<b>Calendar</b>
<ul>
<li>Month, Week, Work Week, 3 Days, Day and Agenda views</li>
<li>Create, edit and move events, including repeating ones, with reminders</li>
<li>Plan tasks into the hours of the calendar by drag and drop</li>
<li>The phone's other calendars (Google, Exchange and the like), read-only</li>
</ul>

<b>Sync</b>
<ul>
<li>Local-first: works offline and sends changes when the server is reachable</li>
<li>Changes from other devices arrive within seconds through UnifiedPush (with the ntfy app, for instance) and Nextcloud's DAV Push; no Google services</li>
<li>Several Nextcloud accounts side by side; sign in with the browser or an app password</li>
<li>Preferences sync between devices, also with Todav on the GNOME desktop</li>
</ul>

<b>On Android</b>
<ul>
<li>Material 3 Expressive, with a sidebar on unfolded foldables and tablets</li>
<li>Home-screen widgets: a task list, the month, the agenda, a day's hours and a month planner</li>
<li>"New Task" from the launcher and Quick Settings, sharing text as a task, keyboard shortcuts</li>
</ul>
